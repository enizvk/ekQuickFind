## 📦 Get it

Grab the latest APK from [Releases](https://github.com/enizvk/ekQuickFind/releases/latest). It keeps itself up to date: it checks once a day on Wi-Fi and installs new versions after you allow it once.

Requires Android 17 or newer. ekQuickFind is free and open source.

## 🧩 Features

### 🔎 Search everything
- **Apps**, including work profile apps, with app shortcuts and uninstall/info actions
- **Contacts** with call and message buttons
- **Calendar**: events by title or place, upcoming first, with day, time and place
- **Device settings**: about 80 settings screens, plus live switches for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Do Not Disturb, auto-rotate, battery saver and the flashlight
- **Files and media**: photos, videos, music, and documents and downloads with All files access
- **Photo tags**: find photos by what's in them and by keywords saved in them, tagged on the phone in the background
- **Quick answers**: a calculator and unit conversion for length, weight, volume, temperature, time, data and speed
- **Web**: live suggestions from Google (default), DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave or your own engine, plus a "Search the web" row; web search can be turned off entirely
- **Ask AI**: hand what you typed to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini in one tap

### ⚡ Shortcuts, translation and weather
- **Site shortcuts**: start with a keyword to search YouTube (`yt`), Google Translate (`tr`), Google Maps (`map`), Play Store (`play`), Wikipedia (`wiki`) or Reddit (`r`), opening the app when it's installed. Matching words work too, like "youtube cats"
- **Your own sites**: add any site with a keyword and a search address, and rename any keyword
- **Your list, your call**: turn sites on or off, delete the ones you never use, and bring them back with Restore default sites. Wikipedia, Reddit and Weather start off
- **Keyword separator**: plain `yt cats` by default, or set your own, such as `:` for `yt: cats`
- **The keyword lights up** in the search bar with the site's icon, so you know a shortcut is about to run
- **Translation card**: `tr` followed by text shows the translation in its own group, with the languages it went between. Tap to copy, or open it in Google Translate
- **Pick the language** to translate into, and the translator: Google's web translator, or on the phone and offline after a one-time download
- **Weather card**: `weather sofia`, `w sofia` or just `weather` for where you are, in °C or °F

### 🕘 Before you type
- **Recently opened items** as a list, or as a strip of chips that sits inside the panel before you type and next to it while you type. Show 1 to 20 (5 by default)
- **Clear recents** with the small X in the strip. What you use most still ranks first
- **Pinned favourites** as a grid of icons
- **Smart suggestions**: what you usually open at this hour, or with the headphones or car that are connected
- **Next up**: the event happening now or in the next 12 hours
- **Clipboard chip**: copied a link or a number? Tap the chip to search it, open the link, call the number or share it, and swipe back through the last 10 clips you opened this week. The clipboard is only read when you tap
- Each of these has its own switch

### 🧠 Smart results
- Typo-tolerant matching that highlights the letters that matched, including initials: "gm" finds Google Maps
- An exact-match button in the search bar for whole words only (and a switch to hide it)
- The best match is selected, so Enter opens it; arrow keys move the selection on a hardware keyboard
- Results you open rank higher over time
- Pin favourites, hide results and exclude apps
- "More below" names the sections that are still out of view

### 🎨 Make it yours
- Sections you can reorder by dragging, each shown as rows, a compact grid or chips
- A text size slider and five fonts (Lora, JetBrains Mono, Roboto Condensed and Nunito are built in, so they work with any system font style) in four weights
- The best match in its own section with an animated highlight, or as a big card on top
- A tinted, soft-glow or gradient highlight on the active result, with its own animation (settle, lift or a border glow)
- A gentle animation on the search bar when it opens: light sweep, colour pulse, shimmer ring, breathe or twin lights
- Search bar at the bottom or top, with the selected result kept right above it for one-handed use
- Frosted glass, blur or solid background with adjustable dim and blur strength, including Samsung's own blur on One UI
- Light, dark or system theme, wallpaper colours and ten accent colours
- Haptics, reduced motion and smooth Material 3 Expressive animations

### 🧭 Friendly settings
- Search settings by name and jump straight to the option
- A live preview pinned on Look and feel that follows every change, with pictures instead of words for layouts and styles
- Quick looks (Minimal, Balanced, Showcase) to start from
- "More options" keeps the rare stuff out of the way, and an ⓘ explains the trickier options
- A dot marks panels you've changed, each with its own Reset, plus Reset all settings in one tap (your history, pins and hidden results stay)
- A friendly first start: allow everything ekQuickFind can search in one go, no digging through system settings

### 🚪 Open it from anywhere
- A floating button over every app: drag it anywhere and it settles on the nearest edge; size and transparency are adjustable
- App icon, home screen search bar widget and Quick Settings tile
- Launcher shortcuts for pinned results
- Set it as your digital assistant, or as the system search for other apps' search buttons

### 🛡️ Private and up to date
- Everything stays on the phone: no accounts, analytics or ads, and history is left out of backups
- One tap clears history
- The direct-download version checks GitHub once a day and updates itself

## 🔒 Private by design

- History, pins and settings stay on your phone. No accounts, no analytics, no ads.
- Only web suggestions, the weather and translation cards and the daily update check (direct-download version) use the network. Each can be turned off, and web search can be switched off entirely.
- Photo tags are worked out on the phone; no photo ever leaves it.
- Smart suggestions are learned from your own history, on the phone.
- Weather asks [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) only when you search for weather. "weather" with no place uses your approximate location, which is never stored.
- Translation sends only the text after `tr` to Google's web translator, after a short pause in typing. Choose "On this phone" to translate offline with Google ML Kit instead; nothing leaves the phone then.
- The clipboard is only read when you tap the Clipboard chip. The clips you open are kept on the phone for 7 days, never in backups, and Clear history removes them. Text Android marks as sensitive, like passwords, is never read or kept.
- History is left out of cloud backups unless you turn that on.

## 💡 Tips

- **Faster than the icon:** add the Quick Settings tile or the home screen search bar from *Settings > Ways to open*.
- **One-handed:** turn on *Selected result by the search bar* so the best match sits right above the keyboard.
- **Translate into your language:** pick it in *Settings > Web and AI > Translation*, then type `tr` and the text.
- **Copied an address?** Open search and tap the Clipboard chip to open it or search it.
- **In the car:** open the same music or maps app a couple of times with the car connected, and it's suggested every time you're back in the car.
- **Samsung:** set the side button double press to open ekQuickFind (*Settings > Advanced features > Side button*).

## 🛠️ For developers
